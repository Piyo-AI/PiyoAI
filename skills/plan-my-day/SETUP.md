# Set up plan my day

Nothing to set up: no accounts, keys, folders or internet access.

## Try it

1. Say: "Plan my day. I have a dentist appointment at 2pm, a report to finish, and I'm low on energy after lunch."
2. Piyo checks the time and weekday, asks for anything missing, and answers with a short timed plan. It leaves
   gaps for meals and breaks and tells you what did not fit.

## What this skill can do

- It only writes a plan from what you tell it. It asks for no tools beyond the always-available clock, so it
  cannot read your calendar or change anything. To plan around your real calendar, ask for the morning brief or
  use the Calendar skill.
