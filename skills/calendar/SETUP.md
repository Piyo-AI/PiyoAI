# Connect Google Calendar

Calendar uses the same Google connection as Gmail and the morning brief. If you already connected Google, you
may only need to allow calendar changes.

## One Google connection

If you have not created a Google OAuth client yet, follow the full guide first. It covers both Gmail and
Calendar.

:::open-setup gmail-triage:::

- [ ] Google is set up (or I am following the full guide)

## Allow what you want

Reading your calendar is included. To let Piyo add, move or delete events, tick **Add, change and delete calendar
events** and choose **Add the selected access**. Every change shows you a preview (title, time and time zone,
and who gets an invitation) and waits for you to approve it.
