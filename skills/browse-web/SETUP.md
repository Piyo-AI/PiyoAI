# Set up Browse the web

Piyo opens pages in its own browser, separate from the one you use every day. It has its own cookies and
history, so it is not signed in to anything of yours.

## Install Piyo's browser (once)

1. Ask Piyo to look at a page, for example: "Open example.com in the browser and tell me what it says."
2. The first time, a banner says Piyo's browser needs a one-time download (about 150 MB). Press **Install browser**
   and wait for the progress bar to finish. It is downloaded by Playwright, the tool Piyo uses to drive the
   browser, and stored in your user folder.
3. Ask again. Piyo opens the page and reads it.

## Things worth knowing

- While Piyo uses the browser a banner shows **Piyo is using its browser** with the site. **Show browser** opens
  the window so you can watch; **Close pages** closes what Piyo opened.
- Piyo does not sign in, enter passwords, card or ID numbers, or get past CAPTCHAs. It tells you what it sees and
  you decide.
- To limit where it may go, use **Settings > Browser**: allowed sites, blocked sites and how many pages one
  request may open.

## What this skill can do

- Open public pages and read them, click and type on them (it asks you before anything that sends or submits),
  wait for a page to load, and take a screenshot.
- It needs no accounts and no keys.
