# Set up price compare

Piyo checks a product on a few shops in its own browser and reports the cheapest, with links. It never buys
anything and never signs in.

## One thing to set up

This skill uses Piyo's own browser. If you have not used it before, it needs a one-time download of about 150 MB:
follow the steps in **Browse the web** (Settings > Skills > Browse the web > Setup), or just ask for a
comparison and press **Install browser** when the banner appears.

## Try it

1. Say: "Compare the price of a Logitech MX Master 3S in two shops I use: amazon.com and bestbuy.com."
2. Piyo tells you the plan, visits each shop, and reports price, shipping or tax if the page says, stock, and the
   page address. Check the links before you buy: shops change prices and Piyo reads only what is on the page.

## Things worth knowing

- Name the exact product (brand, model, size, colour, new or used) and the shops. Piyo does not guess shop
  addresses.
- It looks at up to four shops and about five pages per shop. **Settings > Browser** also limits how many pages
  one request may open, and can restrict which sites are allowed.
- Cookie banners: Piyo declines non-essential cookies.

## What this skill can do

- Open and read public shop pages, search, click and type on them.
- It needs no accounts or keys.
